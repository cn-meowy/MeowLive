MeowLive Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 18, 2026

The MeowLive team understands the importance of personal information to you and will do its best to protect your privacy. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using the App. By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read, understood and agreed to all of the contents of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection

  1. The App itself does not collect any of your personal information for operating purposes and integrates no statistics, analytics or advertising services.
  2. The following data are generated only through your active actions and are stored or transmitted as described in this policy:
    • the server address you enter in Settings;
    • account credentials you actively provide (QR-login authorization, Cookie, username), used for account binding;
    • the follow list you actively save and the watch history you generate.

2. Local Storage

  1. Preferences such as the server address and danmaku settings are stored only locally on your device, using the local-storage mechanisms provided by the operating system.
  2. Your follow list, watch history and account credentials are transmitted to the self-configured server you designate for storage; the developers do not access or store such data.
  3. When you uninstall the App, the local data described above are removed together with the App; data already transmitted to your server must be managed or deleted through that server.

3. Transmission of Account Credentials

  1. Credentials involved in account binding (QR login, Cookie entry, username entry) are transmitted only to the server you configure in Settings, which handles the account capabilities.
  2. Please ensure the server you configure is safe and reliable. The developers are not responsible for any credential leakage or data loss caused by issues with that server.
  3. You can clear the account credentials of any site at any time in "Settings → Account Management" within the App.

4. Network Access

  1. The App requires network access to request content lists and playback URLs from the server you configure, to receive danmaku relayed by it, and to load cover images and audio/video streams directly from the addresses returned by the server.
  2. The App does not transmit your usage data to any statistics or analytics service.

5. Third-Party Services

  1. Third-party content you browse through the server is subject to the privacy policies of the respective platforms.
  2. The App is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party platforms; please refer to each platform's privacy policy.

6. Protection of Minors

  1. The App is not intended to provide services specifically for minors and does not actively collect personal information from minors.
  2. Minors should use the App under the guidance of a guardian, who shall bear the corresponding supervisory responsibility.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  1. The developers reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time without further notice.
  2. After the policy is changed, your continued use of the App shall be deemed acceptance of the modified Privacy Policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developers at meowycn@163.com.

If you do not agree with any part of this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App. Use of the App indicates that you have fully understood and agreed to the contents above.
